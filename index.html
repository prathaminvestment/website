Mutual Fund Categories – Smart Investing Framework

Select the right mutual fund category based on your goals, time horizon, and risk appetite — not short-term performance.

Large Cap Funds

Suitable for investors seeking stability and steady long-term growth through well-established companies.

Risk: ●●●○○ Horizon: 5+ years

Mid Cap Funds

Ideal for investors with moderate to high risk appetite aiming for higher growth potential.

Risk: ●●●●○ Horizon: 7+ years

Flexi Cap Funds

Dynamic allocation across market caps, adapting to changing market conditions.

Risk: ●●●○○ Horizon: 5–7 years

Multi Cap Funds

Diversified equity exposure across large, mid and small cap stocks.

Risk: ●●●●○ Horizon: 7+ years

Small Cap Funds

High growth potential with higher volatility, suitable for aggressive long-term investors.

Risk: ●●●●● Horizon: 10+ years

Multi Asset Funds

Invest across equity, debt and gold to reduce volatility and improve stability.

Risk: ●●○○○ Horizon: 4–6 years

Aggressive Hybrid Funds

Balanced mix of equity and debt aiming for growth with controlled risk.

Risk: ●●●○○ Horizon: 5+ years
